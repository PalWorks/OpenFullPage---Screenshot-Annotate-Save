How the health score is calculated

1

Weekly active seats

Seats that opened a report in the last seven days, over seats billed. A workspace paying for 300 and using 40 is counted as using 40.

2

Depth of use

How many of the six core surfaces a workspace touched. Breadth predicts renewal better than raw session count does.

3

Support load

Open tickets weighted by age. A quiet account is not always a happy one, so silence after a spike counts against the score.

health = (0.5 * activeSeatRatio) + (0.3 * surfaceBreadth) - (0.2 * weightedTickets)

Notes for the board

What moved

Week three carried the month. A single partner introduction closed four workspaces inside six days, which is the peak on the chart above and is also why the expansion line barely moved: none of the four had an account with us before September.

Eastcliff Mutual is the one number worth reading twice. Their seat count has not changed, and their usage has halved since the July reorganisation. The renewal date is 14 January, which leaves one quarter to fix it.

Asks

  • A decision on the Starter tier, which now costs more to serve than it earns.

Method

Figures are drawn from the billing ledger rather than the analytics pipeline, so they will not tie exactly to the product dashboards. Revenue is recognised on invoice date, and anything refunded inside the period is removed rather than netted off the following month.
